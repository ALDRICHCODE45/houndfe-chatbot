Branch: `feat/smoke-test-improvements`. Backend/frontend parts (catalog search matching, POS request context N2, detected receipt amount N9) were handed to the backend session; they are not in this document.

## Specs

- **S1 — Owner (verbatim):** "iniciemos con todas las mejoras que habiamos anotado anteriormente, bajemoslas bien primero y despues las implementamos." Order chosen: "Conversación primero" (N1, N4, N5, N7) → N11 search → technical.
- **S2 — N1 cart summary invites to continue.** Owner (verbatim): "Piensa en una persona hablando normalmente, el bot en este mensaje no te da siguientes pasos, solo te dice que se hizo". Decision: the cart summary (`minimal-cart.service.ts:658`, ends "No incluye envío y no reserva existencias. Todavía no se ha creado un pedido.") ends with `¿Desea agregar algo más o seguimos con su compra?` and two interactive reply buttons `Seguir con la compra` and `Agregar algo más`. usted/tú styling applies. Tapping `Seguir con la compra` behaves like the customer asking to buy (starts checkout: delivery data / shipping or pickup, exactly as "quiero comprar" does today); `Agregar algo más` invites them to say which product. Stale buttons (cart changed) follow the existing stale-button pattern.
- **S3 — N5 order summary.** The shipping cost / ETA appear once (today the shipping block "Su envío al código postal X no tiene costo. Llega en aproximadamente N día hábil." precedes a summary that repeats them: `minimal-catalog-agent.service.ts:532-536`, `domain/minimal-order-confirmation.ts:317-327`), and the summary always ends with `Total a pagar: $X MXN` (products + customer shipping charge; free shipping included) before the SÍ prompt (today only added when `customerChargeCents !== 0`, `minimal-order-confirmation.ts:330-336`). Pickup summaries keep their pickup text and also show `Total a pagar`.
- **S4 — N4 zone approved resumes checkout.** Owner (verbatim): "Pídame de nuevo el envío le pasa el trabajo al cliente". Decision: when staff approve a delivery zone, instead of `deliveryZoneResolvedText` (`delivery-zones/domain/delivery-zone-requester.ts:156`, "… Pídame de nuevo el envío y le muestro el costo actualizado."), the bot sends `¡Listo! Ya tenemos entrega a su código postal {cp}: {shipping sentence}.` and immediately re-quotes shipping and sends the order summary with the confirm button, only when the customer's confirmed delivery draft still has that ZIP and the cart is non-empty, inside the 24 h window, respecting the cart CAS writes. If any precondition fails (cart empty/changed, ZIP changed, quote fails, outside window), fall back to the current notice text without the resume. Rejected zones / CARRIER_ONLY keep their current behaviour.
- **S5 — N7 summary vs previous order.** "Muéstrame el resumen de mi pedido" (and similar: "mi resumen", "ver mi pedido") with a pending cart/summary re-shows the current summary (or cart), and with a recently placed sale shows that order (folio, total, payment status/next step); only "pedido anterior / la última vez / lo mismo que la vez pasada" uses `getLastOrder`. Fix the prompt overlap (`minimal-catalog-agent.service.ts:495`, `:1579-1581`, `:645`) and add a deterministic route in `handleTurn` when a pending summary or placed sale exists.
- **S6 — Paid orders.** When the receipt outcome poller observes a CONFIRMED receipt with `sale.debtCents === 0` (`receipt-media/domain/receipt-outcome.ts:91-96`), the bot marks the placed sale paid in the conversation state (`placedSale`, `minimal-cart.service.ts:32-49`, backward compatible). A paid placed sale no longer offers payment methods/details; a payment question answers that the order is already paid (reuse `Su pedido ya está pagado.` styling) and never creates a team request.
- **S7 — N11 search broadening (bot side).** When `searchCatalog` returns 0 results for a multi-word query, the bot retries automatically (bounded, e.g. at most 3 extra backend calls) with the significant words alone — dropping generic words like "croquetas", "alimento", "para", "de", "comida" — and merges results (dedupe by productId, keep backend order). The model is told when results came from a broadened search so it says e.g. "Croquetas Pedigree no tenemos; de Pedigree tenemos …". Port the "one broader refinement" guidance from the legacy sale-flow prompt to the minimal route. Out-of-stock matches are still shown as alternatives with their status.
- **S8 — Technical.** (a) `CONVERSATION_MIRROR_DELIVERY_ENABLED` is on for any value Joi accepts as true (`true`, `TRUE`, `True`, …) — configuration and validation agree (`configuration.ts:34`, `env.validation.ts:175`); same check for other boolean env flags read with `=== 'true'`. (b) The conversation mirror downloader logs a `warn` with the safe failure reason (same side channel as receipts) when a Meta media download fails; no URL/token/media id.
- **S10 — Catalog relevance (prod follow-up, 2026-11-17).** Owner (verbatim): "la segunda opcion que me paso (croquetas Naturaels sin grano de salmon y camote, es para perror adultos de raza grande, no para cachorros, me pregunto.. como llego a esa conclusion de que esas croquetas me servirian aunque le hubiera dicho especificamente croquetas para cachorro" / "si en algun momento salen muchaaas croquetas cuando ponga "croquetas" me las va a pasar todas aunque muy probablemente muchas de ellas no sean de "cachorro" como pedi en mi mensaje??". Evidence: one search, `searchResultCount=2`, no broadening; backend OR-matched "cachorro" (Nupec Cachorro) and "croquetas" (Naturales … para Perros Adultos de Raza Grande); the model offered both. Rules: (a) the bot offers as options only products consistent with every attribute the customer stated (life stage cachorro/adulto/senior, species perro/gato, brand, breed size, flavour/type when stated); a product whose name/description contradicts a stated attribute is never presented as a match — at most mentioned once, separately, as a different alternative ("también tengo para adulto, si le sirve"); (b) at most 3 product options per reply; when more relevant products exist, the bot asks one narrowing question (size, brand, presentation) instead of listing them; (c) when no result satisfies the request, it says so plainly and offers the closest alternative labelled as such; (d) when the backend adds an optional per-item match flag (`match: 'all' | 'partial'` or similar, requested 2026-11-17), partial matches are treated as alternatives, never as matches — the parser must ignore the field until then and accept it when present. Deterministic where possible (cap and partial flag in code); attribute contradiction judged by the model with explicit prompt rules and tests on the tool-result shape/prompt.
- **S11 — Out-of-stock options (N13, prod 2026-11-17).** Evidence: "croquetas pedigree manejan?" offered "Pedigree Adulto Trozos de Res en Salsa" lata $49 as an alternative while it has 0 units (POS "Sin stock"); the search result strips stock and the model did not call checkStock. Owner choice (verbatim): "Mostrarlo marcado como agotado". Rule: before the customer sees options, every offered product (≤3, incl. alternatives) carries a deterministic availability status taken from code, not from the model's choice to call a tool; an out-of-stock product is shown explicitly as agotado (with variants: say which presentations are agotadas) and the bot offers the existing restock flow (ask the team when it arrives); it is never presented as available, never added to the cart as available. Products with stock are preferred: an agotado product does not take the place of an available match. Respect the stock-evidence boundary already in place (read odd/tasks/inventory-evidence-boundary.md, stock-conversation-boundary*.md): if fresh stock evidence is required, fetch it in code for the ≤3 shown products in parallel; if the search payload's stock status may be used for an "agotado" label, prefer it (zero extra backend calls — owner directive: performance first). Document which one and why.
- **S12 — Availability from search, no extra calls (2026-11-17).** Owner (verbatim): "por que dices que el 2 mas adelante?? por que no ahora? por que posponerlo?" then «Sí, chatbot + los 2 arreglos del backend». Backend evidence (intercom 2026-11-17): search and /catalog/:id/stock read the same live columns, no cache/replica, same `deriveStockState` thresholds, single balance per tenant, both exclude expired lots and OFF variants; they differ only for products WITH variants: search product-level status = best visible variant status but quantity = raw product.quantity (misleading); /stock product-level status/quantity ignore variants (wrong). Rule: S11 availability labels come from the search payload (no getStock calls per search): simple or not-managed product → `stock.status` of search; product with variants → each variant's status (product-level status from search is acceptable as aggregate); never use product-level quantity for products with variants from either endpoint. Update the inventory-evidence boundary docs to record that search stock status is live evidence for availability labels. Remove the per-search getStock reads added in T7 (a263816); keep agotado ordering, labels, restock offer. checkStock (explicit tool) keeps working; for products with variants it must rely on variant statuses, not product-level ones (until backend fixes /stock). Backend asked to fix both endpoints.
- **S13 — Code-rendered catalog options (2026-11-17).** Evidence (prod, after deploys): for "croquetas pedigree manejan?" the model listed Pedigree Dentastix (treats, same brand) BEFORE "[PRUEBA] Croquetas Naturales…" (same type, other brand), did not label "de otra marca", and closed with «Si buscas las croquetas de Pedigree, con gusto averiguo cuándo nos vuelve a llegar» while the agotado product is "Pedigree Adulto Trozos de Res en Salsa Lata 375 g" and no Pedigree croquetas exist (false restock promise). Owner (verbatim): «si hermano.» to: the code builds the options message; the model keeps deciding what to search and the conversation. Rules: (a) when a catalog search yields products to present, the customer-facing options message (list, labels, closing) is rendered by code from the tool result, not written by the model (follow the existing fixed-reply pattern, e.g. askTeam/expiration styled replies); usted/tú styling via customer-style. (b) Classification from data the bot has: significant query tokens (T4 stopword logic) split into brand tokens (equal to some item's normalized `brand`) and type tokens (the rest); each item is exact (backend match 'all', or every significant token present in name/brand/description), same-type (a type token in name/description), brand-only (brand token matches, no type token) or other. Order of offered options (≤3 total, available first): exact → same-type (suffix «(de otra marca)» when a brand was requested and the item's brand differs) → brand-only (last; omitted when 3 better options exist). Agotado products (S11/S12 availability) never offered as available: one separate line «Además, tengo {nombre exacto}, pero está agotado.» (presentations named for variants). (c) Opening line: exact matches → «Encontré estas opciones:»; no exact but alternatives → «{lo pedido} no tengo, pero tengo estas opciones:» where {lo pedido} is the customer's significant words in their order (e.g. «Croquetas Pedigree no tengo, …»); nothing → current no-result behaviour. (d) Closing: available options + agotado + restock flow available → «¿Cuál le gustaría llevar? Y si la que busca es {nombre exacto del agotado}, con gusto le averiguo cuándo nos vuelve a llegar.»; available only → «¿Cuál le gustaría llevar?»; only agotado + restock → «{nombre exacto} está agotado. ¿Quiere que le pregunte al equipo cuándo vuelve a llegar?»; restock not available → no restock promise. The restock offer always names the exact agotado product and arms the existing prepareRestock path for that productId only. (e) Each option line: bold name, short description, presentations with prices (current WhatsApp formatting); product image behaviour unchanged; presented-products registry unchanged so checkStock/cart keep working. (f) More relevant products than shown → one fixed narrowing question instead of the closing list question. (g) Model still handles non-catalog turns, clarifications, cart, checkout; prompt fragments that told the model how to write options/closings are trimmed so they do not conflict.
- **S9 — Compatibility and evidence.** Stored state written before this change keeps working. Every rule has a RED test first; existing behaviour touched (cart, checkout, shipping, receipt outcome) keeps its tests green. Docs describing changed replies are updated.
