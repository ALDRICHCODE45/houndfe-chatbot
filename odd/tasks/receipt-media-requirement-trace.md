## Scope and status semantics

- **Scope:** trace only. No source, test, spec, migration, config, or package change accompanies this ledger, and ODD-7A does not close ODD-7 or ODD-8.
- **Keys:** every row has exactly one ledger-local key `<capability>/R<n>/S<n>` in source order. Historical informal codes (WU*/ODD-*) appear only as evidence references, never as canonical scenario ids.
- **`verified`** — a current test body named in the row asserts the scenario's clauses. Old OpenSpec checkboxes, design prose, and mere existence of the committed runbook are never sufficient; documentation-owned scenarios whose runbook content is deferred or unasserted are `unverified` (see G-3/G-4).
- **`unverified`** — no current matching assertion or recorded run supports at least one clause. The gap register explains each.
- **`skipped`** — intentionally not asserted because the historical scenario is explicitly superseded or a duplicate, never silently omitted; each skipped key records its disposition, authoritative replacement, or duplicate owner in the gap register.
- **`N-A`** — the scenario truly requires owner infrastructure/provisioning; implementable scenarios are never relabelled `N-A`.
- **Implementation-complete vs rollout-ready:** this ledger measures implementation/test traceability only. Owner-gated DigitalOcean Spaces, staging drills, and production enablement live in ODD-8 and are not counted as coverage here.
- **Established environmental baseline (unchanged by this slice):** `pnpm exec tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.spec.json` exits 2 with exactly 95 unrelated spec diagnostics and zero references to the then-candidate paths; Docker-gated suites require `RUN_DOCKER_TESTS=1`; `RECEIPT_MEDIA_INGESTION_ENABLED` and `DATABASE_URL` remain unset.
- **OpenSpec immutability:** `openspec/changes/receipt-media-ingestion/**` has no tracked diff and was not edited, staged, or reformatted by ODD-7A.
